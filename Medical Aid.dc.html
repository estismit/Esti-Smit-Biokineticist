Does Medical Aid Cover Biokinetics?

In most cases, Biokinetics sessions are paid from your medical savings account.

However, certain medical aid schemes and benefit options provide additional cover for Biokinetics, rehabilitation or specific health management programmes.

Coverage depends on your medical aid, your specific plan and your available benefits.

Not sure what your medical aid covers? Get in touch with us and we'll help you understand which benefits to enquire about.
